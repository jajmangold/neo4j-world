# Narrative Governors

The world sim should preserve continuity and generate pressure. It should not create chaos.

## Core Governors

- Scandal cooldowns: major public reveals need downtime before another major reveal.
- Escalation budget: every arc has limited escalation per episode.
- Emotional rhythm: contrast high-tension beats with humor, tenderness, status, fear, or embarrassment.
- Arc spacing: do not resolve every active arc in the same episode.
- Rarity rules: pregnancy, death, prison, public ruin, and major violence should be rare.
- Consequence persistence: large events should alter future prompts, wardrobe, posture, reputation, and relationships.

## Episode Shape

A short episode should normally do one main thing:

- reveal a small secret
- worsen a relationship
- create a near-miss
- shift status
- introduce a new pressure
- pay off a previous hook

## Anti-Patterns

- every scene is a betrayal
- every relationship is equally tense
- every character has too many secrets
- events happen without visible consequences
- characters change emotions without a causal bridge
